## How to use

1. **Choose an image.** Drag and drop a JPG, PNG, or WebP photo onto the upload box, or click **Upload Image**. You can also click **Try Sample** to test with the authentic Jaipur Palace photograph.
2. **Select your Enlarge Scale.** Choose **2× (200% HD)**, **4× (400% Ultra HD / Print)**, or enter custom target dimensions in pixels. Proportions are preserved automatically.
3. **Enhance with Photoshop-Grade Quality.**
   - Click **⚡ 1-Click Auto Enhance** for automatic tone curve, exposure, dynamic range, and vibrancy balance.
   - Or adjust the 5 precision sliders manually: **Sharpness & Clarity**, **Contrast**, **Brightness & Exposure**, **Vibrance**, and **Warmth**.
4. **Compare & Download.** Drag the interactive **Before / After** comparison slider to inspect fine edge details, texture, and color richness. Select your preferred output format (PNG, JPG, WebP, or PDF) and download your enlarged image directly.

## Method

**100% In-Browser Privacy.** No files or images are ever uploaded to remote servers. All resampling, bicubic interpolation, unsharp masking, S-curve contrast adjustments, and color grading happen locally in your browser using high-speed Canvas and WebAssembly.

**High-Fidelity Enlarging.** Scaling photos up by 200% or 400% uses multi-stage bicubic interpolation coupled with edge-preserving unsharp masking. This avoids pixelation, blurriness, and artifacts common in naive image stretchers.

**Photoshop Camera Raw Precision.**
- **Sharpness & Clarity:** Uses an unsharp mask convolution kernel to recover micro-contrast along edges, fabrics, architectural lines, and portrait details without producing harsh halos.
- **Contrast & Dynamic Range:** Employs sigmoidal S-curve tone mapping to deepen shadows and clarify highlights without clipping highlights to pure white or crushing blacks.
- **Brightness & Exposure:** Applies smooth gamma curve transformation to lift underexposed shots naturally.
- **Saturation & Vibrance:** Intelligently intensifies muted tones while protecting skin tones from oversaturation.
- **Color Warmth:** Fine-tunes white balance across the Kelvin spectrum from cool studio tones to golden sunlight.

## FAQ

### Is Image Enlarger & Enhancer free?

Yes. It is 100% free with no sign-up, no subscriptions, no credit card required, and no watermarks added.

### Are my personal photos uploaded to a cloud server?

No. Unlike cloud-based AI upscalers that send your private photos to remote servers, this tool executes all enlargement and quality enhancement entirely inside your browser. Your images never leave your computer or phone.

### What is the difference between 2x and 4x enlarge?

- **2× Scale (200%)** doubles pixel dimensions (e.g., 1080p becomes 4K UHD), perfect for high-resolution displays, e-commerce stores, and digital banners.
- **4× Scale (400%)** quadruples pixel dimensions (e.g., 720p becomes 4K, or 1080p becomes 8K), ideal for high-DPI prints, posters, and framed wall art.

### How does the 1-Click Auto Enhance feature work?

Auto Enhance analyzes the luminance histogram of your photograph, expands dynamic range, recovers shadows, and introduces subtle clarity and vibrance, replicating the professional Camera Raw workflow in one click.
