## How to use

1. **Open or drop one or more `.docx` files**, or select **Sample DOCX** to try two documents
   generated in your browser.
2. Choose the page size (A4 or Letter) and the margins, and whether to make one combined PDF or one
   PDF per document.
3. Select **Create PDF**, then **Download PDF**. The summary shows the real page count and file size.

## Method

**What is converted.** The `.docx` file is opened in your browser and its text is laid out on PDF
pages: title and heading sizes, bold and italic, bulleted list items, line breaks and page breaks.
Each table row is written as one line with its cells separated by "|". Text wraps to the page width
and flows onto new pages as needed.

**What is not converted.** Images, text boxes, headers, footers, footnotes, colours and the exact Word
layout and fonts are not carried over; the PDF uses the standard Helvetica font family. Those fonts
cover Western European text, so other characters — for example Hindi, or the ₹ sign — are shown
as "?", and the page tells you how many. Only `.docx` files are supported, not the older `.doc`
format.

**Browser-based processing.** The documents are read and the PDF is written on your own device;
nothing is uploaded.

## FAQ

### Is Word to PDF free to use?

Yes. It is free, with no sign-up and no watermark.

### Will my PDF look exactly like the Word document?

No. The PDF keeps the text and its structure, but not the exact layout, fonts or images. For an
exact copy, use "Save as PDF" in Word or Google Docs.

### Can it convert .doc files?

No. Open the file in Word or LibreOffice and save it as `.docx` first.

### Are my documents uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
