## How to use

1. **Upload or select your Word file** (`.docx` or `.doc`), or click **Load Sample**.
2. **Choose page setup**: Select standard paper sizes (A4, US Letter, Legal) and set margin preferences (Normal, Narrow, Wide).
3. **Configure options**: Enable or disable automatic header and footer page numbering.
4. Click **Convert & Download PDF**. The formatted PDF will be generated immediately on your device.

## Method

**In-Browser Document Engine.** Word document XML markup is parsed and mapped directly to PDF page streams using pure client-side libraries.

**Zero Data Leakage.** Your business files, resumes, and academic manuscripts never leave your computer or phone.

## FAQ

### Is Word to PDF free?

Yes. It is 100% free with no subscription and no usage caps.

### Are Word files converted with accurate formatting?

Yes. Standard paragraph styles, headings, tables, and page setups are rendered faithfully into vector PDF pages.

### Do I need Microsoft Word installed on my machine?

No. The conversion is performed directly by the browser without needing MS Office or third-party software.
