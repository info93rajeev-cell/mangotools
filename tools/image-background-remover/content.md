## How to use

1. **Upload your image.** Click the prominent **Upload Image** button, drag and drop any JPG, PNG, or WebP photo, or click **Try Sample** to test with an authentic example photograph.
2. **Tune removal settings.** Choose an automatic solid background detection, click anywhere on the visual canvas stage with the eye-dropper to pick a custom background color, or adjust the **Color Tolerance** (1–100%) and **Edge Smoothing** feathering (0–10 px).
3. **Select output format & download.** Choose **PNG (Transparent)** for clean transparent cutouts, **WebP** for compact file size, **JPG** with a solid white or custom background, or **PDF** for high-demand official documents. Click **Download transparent image** to save your result instantly.

## Method

**In-browser pixel segmentation.** Background removal executes completely on your device using typed array color-distance analysis and subpixel anti-aliasing. Euclidean distance metrics across red, green, and blue color spaces isolate background pixels without sending private photo data across the internet.

**Edge feathering and smoothing.** Sharp hard cutouts often leave jagged pixel steps around subject boundaries. The configurable edge feathering filter applies a smooth alpha interpolation across edge boundaries, delivering studio-quality translucent outlines around hair, clothing, and curved silhouettes.

**High-demand PDF document output.** When preparing passport cutouts, ID photos, or product assets for official portals, users frequently need PDF documents. This tool embeds high-resolution cutouts into clean PDF documents on demand via client-side code-splitting.

**Zero uploads privacy guarantee.** Unlike third-party online tools that send your photos to remote AI cloud servers, this tool processes every single byte directly inside your browser's local sandbox memory. No images are saved, tracked, or uploaded.

## FAQ

### How do I remove an image background for free?

Click **Upload Image** or drag your photo onto the stage. The tool instantly isolates the background. Adjust the tolerance slider if needed, then click **Download transparent image**.

### What image formats can I export?

You can download your cutout as a transparent PNG, compact transparent WebP, solid JPG (with white or custom background), or an official PDF document.

### Are my images uploaded to any server?

No. 100% of the image reading, pixel keying, and re-encoding occurs locally inside your web browser. Your photos never leave your device.

### Can I choose a specific background color to remove?

Yes. Click anywhere on the visual stage canvas to pick that exact color as the background target, or enter a hex color code in the controls.

### Can I replace the background with white or black instead of transparent?

Yes. Select White or Black from the replacement options. This is ideal for e-commerce product listings and marketplace requirements.
